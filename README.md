
# **Revuze**

**Revuze** is a self-hosted, scalable feedback management platform that empowers businesses and developers to gather, manage, and analyze feedback across various platforms—while maintaining complete ownership of their data. Whether it’s feedback from a website, email campaign, or a WordPress site, Revuze ensures seamless integration and delivers actionable insights through real-time reporting.

Built with a modern tech stack, Revuze prioritizes scalability, performance, and security while offering advanced monitoring and code quality enforcement.

---

## **Core Features**

- **Customizable Feedback Widget**  
  Easily generate and customize feedback widgets to match your brand’s identity.  
  Integrate them with websites, email campaigns, or WordPress sites effortlessly.

- **Real-Time Feedback Reporting**  
  Analyze feedback in real-time with interactive dashboards, advanced filters, and visual reports.  

- **Data Ownership**  
  Host Revuze on your infrastructure using Docker, ensuring full control over your feedback data.

- **Cross-Platform Integration**  
  Deploy feedback forms across different platforms, including websites, emails, and CMS platforms like WordPress.

- **Monitoring & Alerts**  
  Integrated with **Datadog** for real-time monitoring and alerts, ensuring operational visibility at scale.

- **Enforced Code Quality**  
  Uses **Husky** pre-commit hooks to ensure high-quality code contributions and a consistent development workflow.

## **Tech Stack**

| **Layer**              | **Technology**                                |

## **Why Revuze?**

1. **Full Control Over Your Data**  
   Unlike third-party SaaS platforms, Revuze allows businesses to maintain complete control over their feedback data by hosting it on their own servers.

2. **Seamless Integration**  
   Whether it's embedding a widget on your website or integrating feedback forms into email campaigns, Revuze ensures frictionless deployment across platforms.

3. **Scalable & Secure**  
   Designed to handle large-scale feedback data efficiently while adhering to best practices in security and performance.

4. **Actionable Insights**  
   Gain valuable insights through real-time reports, helping you make data-driven decisions.
